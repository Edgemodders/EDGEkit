# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Adds EDGEmat (`edgemodders.edgemat`), the material editor, and EDGEwave (`edgemodders.edgewave`), the sound studio, on the Marketplace and Open VSX. Both are previews. EDGEmat is Windows x64 only; EDGEwave builds on REDcode.

## 0.2.0

- REDdeck is now EDGEdeck: the pack installs `edgemodders.edgedeck` in place of `edgemodders.reddeck`, on the Marketplace and Open VSX. The pack page's EDGEdeck docs link works again. Modders who have REDdeck keep it until they uninstall it.

## 0.1.0

- First release, a preview: REDcode for Cyberpunk 2077, REDdeck, the REDnode HUD theme, and REDscript IDE on the Marketplace. REDcode and REDdeck are Windows x64 only.
